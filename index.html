About Us

Your dedicated partner for web & digital growth

Babaro is a web development studio focused on one thing: building digital products that actually get used and deliver results. We combine clean design, solid code, and clear strategy from day one.

Every project starts with understanding your business goals, not just following trends. The result is a website or platform that looks great and works hard to hit your targets.

We've worked with retail brands, F&B businesses, agencies, and B2B companies — each with different goals, but the same need for a digital presence that's fast, clear, and easy to manage.

FAQ

Common questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Reach out and we'll get back to you within a day.

How long does a typical project take?+

Most landing pages take about 1–2 weeks. Company profiles and online stores typically take 3–5 weeks, while custom web apps or platforms can take 6–10 weeks depending on scope.

Do you offer support after launch?+

Yes. Every project includes a support period right after launch, and we offer ongoing maintenance packages for updates, fixes, and monitoring beyond that.

Can you work with our existing brand identity?+

Absolutely. We can design within your existing brand guidelines, or help you develop a simple visual identity if you don't have one yet.

How much does a project cost?+

It depends on scope and complexity. After an initial consultation, we'll send a clear proposal with pricing before any work begins — no hidden costs.
